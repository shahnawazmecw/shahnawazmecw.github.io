About

I am an Assistant Professor at Bennett University, where I teach Machine Learning, Web Technologies, Data Structures, Cloud Computing, and Design and Analysis of Algorithms, and monitor the faculty's SCI(E) publications.

I earned my Ph.D. from the Faculty of Engineering and Technology, Jamia Millia Islamia, New Delhi, with the thesis Securing Cloud-Based Encryption Key Management Platform. Before Bennett I spent eight years as an Assistant Professor at Gurugram University, and began my career as a Lecturer at Gautam Buddha Technical University.

My published work covers cloud key management and cryptography, deep learning for attack detection, federated learning and IoT security, fog and edge computing, intelligent healthcare models, and fuzzy decision methods for software requirements.

Research interests

Cloud security and key management

Secure encryption key management platforms, cloud access security brokers, data deduplication and secure storage.

Applied cryptography

Hybrid and DNA-inspired cryptographic schemes, functional encryption, multi-party computation and image steganography.

Federated learning and IoT security

Blockchain-enhanced federated learning, intrusion and attack detection, and privacy for connected devices.

Fog and edge computing

Architectures, energy-efficient task scheduling and distributed model training for IoT ecosystems.

Intelligent healthcare

Learning models for disease prediction and diagnosis, explainability and secure e-healthcare systems.

Requirements engineering

Fuzzy decision models and recommender systems for software requirements selection, negotiation and prioritization.
